Selected work

Three projects from test stand and fixture design.

Short write-ups of fixture and test-stand work from Redcedar Dynamics, Northline Automation, and a senior-design capstone.

01 / 03

Proof-load fixture

Redcedar Dynamics · Mechanical Engineer · recent work

Redcedar designs custom fixtures and test stands. I was the mechanical designer on a proof-load fixture used to apply a controlled axial load, including the reaction frame, loading hardware, detail drawings, and lab setup support.

What I did

  • Modeled the reaction frame, loading bracket, and alignment hardware in SolidWorks.
  • Applied GD&T on the detail drawings and released the package through SolidWorks PDM.
  • Ran ANSYS structural checks on the fixture frame and critical brackets.
  • Coordinated with the machine shop on fabricated parts.
  • Supported assembly and first test setup in the lab, including fit issues on the floor.

Tools

SolidWorks, SolidWorks PDM, ANSYS, GD&T.

02 / 03

Assembly-line fixtures and packaging-line support

Northline Automation · Junior Mechanical Engineer · 2018–2021

As a junior mechanical engineer, I supported senior staff on fixtures used on an assembly line, and I contributed drawings, BOM updates, and incoming-part inspection notes to a packaging-line improvement project.

What I did

  • Created and updated CAD drawings and bills of materials for automation equipment.
  • Supported fixture design for assembly-line stations.
  • Inspected incoming machined parts and documented fit problems.
  • Tracked open items for the packaging-line work in Excel.

Tools

SolidWorks, AutoCAD, Excel.

03 / 03

Benchtop tensile fixture

Elkhorn State University · Senior design · 2018

Four-person capstone. We designed and built a compact benchtop fixture for tensile testing of small coupons. I owned the load frame and gripping hardware.

What I did

  • Designed the reaction frame and grips in SolidWorks.
  • Prepared the drawing package used in the shop.
  • Helped fabricate and assemble the prototype with the team.

Tools

SolidWorks, basic shop fabrication.
