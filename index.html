About

02 / 02

I am a mechanical engineer in Columbus, Ohio, currently at Redcedar Dynamics, a small shop that designs custom fixtures and test stands. Most of my week is in SolidWorks and on drawings — frames, brackets, load paths, and the details that let a shop actually build the part. The rest is in the lab or on the floor: helping set up a test, checking a fit, or walking a machine shop through a question on a print.

Before Redcedar I spent almost three years at Northline Automation, supporting drawings, bills of materials, and assembly-line fixtures for automation equipment.

I use SolidWorks every day, ANSYS when a frame or bracket needs a structural check, and enough Python to clean up a test plot. I am interested in mechanical and aerospace test-engineering roles — work where the fixture, the stand, and the load path have to be right.
